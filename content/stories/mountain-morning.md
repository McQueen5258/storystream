## The Climb

We started at 4 AM. The air was thin and cold.

![Mountain View](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1464822759023-fed622ff2c3b?auto=format&fit=crop&w=800&q=80)

As we reached the summit, the sun began to rise above the cloud layer. It was a sea of white cotton with golden edges.

### Gear List

* Hiking Boots
* Wool Socks
* Camera (Sony A7III)
* Coffee Thermos

The silence up here is different. It's heavy, yet peaceful.
